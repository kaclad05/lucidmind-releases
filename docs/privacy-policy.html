Key Privacy Highlights

1. Local-First Architecture: Your dreams, voice recordings, journal notes, and personal data remain stored solely on your local device in an isolated SQLite database.

2. User-Initiated AI: Cloud AI processing occurs strictly when you explicitly request dream analysis or interpretation.

3. Zero Model Training: Your dream narratives are never sold, rented, or used to train public machine learning models.

1. Identity & Contact Information

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Lucid Mind (also referred to as "DreamLog", "we", "our", or "us"). The data controller responsible for the application is the Lucid Mind Team.

2. Local-First Data Storage

Lucid Mind is designed with privacy by default. We do not operate central user tracking databases or collect personal profiling data.

  • Dream Journal Entries: Titles, dream descriptions, emotions, lucidity ratings, tags, and dates are stored entirely locally on your device in an encrypted/isolated SQLite database managed via Drift.
  • Audio Recordings: Voice notes and dream audio clips are stored in the application's private device sandbox storage.
  • Device Preferences: Reality check schedules, alarm tones, biometric lock settings, and appearance choices remain stored locally.

We do not have access to your locally stored dreams, audio files, or journal logs.

3. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Processing

Lucid Mind offers advanced dream interpretation, symbolism decoding, and lucidity coaching powered by Google Gemini and Vertex AI language models.

Strict Operating Rules:

  • User-Initiated Only: Dream data is transmitted to the AI service only when you explicitly tap the "Analyze", "Interpret", or "Chat with Dream Guide" actions.
  • Data Minimization: Only the specific dream text submitted for analysis is transmitted. No personally identifying information (such as your name, email, or device IMEI) is transmitted with the prompt.
  • No Model Training: Prompts sent via enterprise API gateways are not utilized to train foundation models without explicit consent.

4. Non-Medical & Educational Disclaimer

Important Medical Notice: Lucid Mind and its AI-generated dream interpretations are strictly intended for self-discovery, entertainment, journaling, and educational purposes. Lucid Mind does NOT provide medical diagnosis, mental health therapy, psychological consultation, or psychiatric treatment.

If you are experiencing severe psychological distress, sleep disorders (e.g. chronic insomnia, sleep apnea), or recurring traumatic nightmares, please consult a qualified healthcare physician or licensed mental health professional.

5. Third-Party Services & Integrations

To provide core application features, Lucid Mind integrates the following vetted third-party service providers:

  • Google Gemini API / Vertex AI: Natural language interpretation of user-submitted dream text.
  • Firebase App Check: Verifies application integrity and protects cloud functions from abuse and unauthorized traffic.
  • Google AdMob: Serves non-intrusive rewarded advertisements for voluntary token replenishment. AdMob may collect standard advertising identifiers subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

6. User Data Rights (GDPR / RODO & CCPA)

Under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you retain the following rights:

  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You can wipe 100% of your data at any time via the in-app feature: Settings → Reset All Data. This immediately deletes the SQLite database, stored files, and caches.
  • Right to Access & Data Portability: You can export your full dream journal to JSON / CSV format via the in-app backup mechanism.
  • Right to Revoke Consent: You may disable AI services or uninstall the application at any time to terminate processing.

7. Children's Privacy

Lucid Mind is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or under 16 within the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children. If we discover that personal data of a minor has been transmitted without parental consent, we will promptly delete it.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product enhancements, legal requirements, or architectural changes. Any material changes will be announced within the application update changelog.
